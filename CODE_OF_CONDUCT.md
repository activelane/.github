# ActiveLane Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ActiveLane aims to provide a thoughtful, welcoming, and technically serious community for people interested in creating and using software.

Participation should be possible regardless of experience, identity, background, nationality, language, disability, appearance, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, or professional status.

## Expected behaviour

Participants are expected to:

- communicate with respect and patience;
- critique ideas and implementations without attacking people;
- assume good faith while remaining open to correction;
- make technical disagreement specific and evidence-based;
- respect privacy, attribution, licences, and confidential disclosures;
- welcome questions without belittling a person’s experience;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- and accept maintainer decisions without harassment or repeated pressure.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discrimination;
- sexualised language or unwanted attention;
- personal insults or sustained hostile communication;
- publishing another person’s private information;
- impersonation or deceptive identity claims;
- deliberate disruption, spam, or coordinated abuse;
- knowingly submitting malicious code or compromised dependencies;
- pressuring people to disclose private security information publicly;
- or retaliating against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in ActiveLane repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, community spaces, events, and private interactions undertaken in an ActiveLane role.

## Reporting

If an incident involves a security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not disclose technical details publicly.

For other conduct concerns, contact an ActiveLane organisation owner privately through an available GitHub contact channel. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Additional private reporting channels will be published as the project grows.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban a participant when reasonably necessary to protect the community.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and impartially as the project’s current capacity permits. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in a review is prohibited.

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers should explain material moderation decisions when doing so does not expose private information or create further harm. This policy applies to maintainers as well as contributors.
